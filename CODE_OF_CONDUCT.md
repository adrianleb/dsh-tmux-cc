# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## Our pledge / 我们的承诺

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

我们承诺为所有参与者提供不受骚扰的协作环境，不因背景、身份、经验水平或个人特征而区别对待。

## Expected behavior / 期望行为

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.

- 保持尊重、建设性和同理心。
- 针对观点和代码提出意见，不攻击个人。
- 尊重不同观点和经验水平。
- 勇于承担责任、道歉并从错误中学习。

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or imagery, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

骚扰、歧视、威胁、色情化语言或图像、蓄意恐吓，以及公开他人隐私信息均不可接受。

## Enforcement / 执行

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is harmful.

如需举报不当行为，请通过仓库所有者的 GitHub 主页私下联系。维护者可编辑或删除不当内容，并可暂时或永久限制造成伤害的参与者。

This policy is based on the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).

本准则基于 [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-cn/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) 制定。
